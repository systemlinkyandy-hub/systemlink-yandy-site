## 5. Creative theme reset: 「まつろわぬ民」

The previous Tanaka-generated visual was a failure and must NOT be used as direction.

Failure mode:
- explanatory poster / self-help aesthetic;
- too much direct text;
- cheap emotional messaging;
- corporate-incident explanation replacing the art;
- loss of botanical / ethnographic / archival dignity and ambiguity.

Correct direction:

「まつろわぬ民」 is NOT a victim poster. It is a study of people / lineages / cultures that are not fully absorbed into a central system and retain another order, memory, movement, body technique, faith or legitimacy.

Visual principles:
- old natural-history plate / ethnographic archive / museum illustration;
- restraint, silence, negative space;
- symbols rather than direct explanation;
- sea-related shrines, coastal beliefs, migration, drift, old maps, tools, plants, butterflies, stone, shell, traces of movement, body technique;
- central authority vs peripheral / autonomous order should be implied, not captioned like an advocacy poster;
- no motivational slogans;
- no chibi characters;
- no "Kei survived" direct messaging inside the plate;
- no cheap meme-poster composition.

The conceptual core is:

> 中央に認定されなくても、別の秩序と正統性を持って生きる者。

Kei recognized that this structure resonates strongly with personal work history, but the artwork should remain broader and generalizable rather than becoming a literal workplace grievance illustration.
